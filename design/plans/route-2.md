# Route 2

## Goal
Build Route 2 as designed in `design/Route2.md`: the coast path south from Carnrudh over a terraced headland, with a lighthouse on its summit and a sandy bay at its foot. Nova battles during a Lumen photo shoot by the lighthouse, and a fisherman at the bay gives the player the Old Rod. It's a breather after Carnrudh's quest, with no Lumen beat. Porthliw is a separate plan; this one stops at the route's far edge.

## Where we're up to (2026-10-15)
- **Phase 1 is done** (commits `ccf8ec806f`, then the rebuild in `80e4bbcc0a`). Route 2 is walkable from Carnrudh, and Carnrudh's survey worker blocks it until the tor is done. The layout is Mossdeep City's terraced hill with real elevation levels; see `design/Route2.md`'s map notes for the coordinates.
- **Next: phase 2** (trainers, items, and the tall grass, which the rebuilt layout doesn't have yet, so Route 2 currently has no wild battles even though `gKerniaRoute2` is set).
- **Before starting phase 2**, read `design/Route2.md` (map notes and levels) and `tools/kernia/gen_kernia_route2.py`. The user tidies layouts in Porymap, so diff `map.bin` against the generator first and copy any hand edits back in (the generator has a `HAND_EDITS` table for one-off cells).
- **Levels gotcha:** the route uses real elevations: 3 for the entry plateau and the middle terraces, 5 for the summit, 7 for the lower terraces and the bay. `layoutlib`'s `GRASS`, `TALL_GRASS`, `STAIRS`, and building stamps are all level 3, so anything placed on the summit or the lower terraces needs its elevation changed to match (as the cottage's is), or the player can't walk onto it. After changing the layout, re-run the level check: with steps blocked, the entry, summit, and bay must be separate regions, and the only level-0 cells are steps.
- **Areas for later phases:** the entry plateau (x 2–17, rows 2–24) and middle terraces (x 11–31, rows 20–32) on level 3; the summit (x 18–30, rows 4–19, level 5) with the lighthouse (x 25–27, rows 5–10) and cottage (x 19–22, rows 7–10); the lower terraces (x 2–24, rows 26–33, level 7); the bay (sand x 0–29, rows 35–41, level 7), with sea rocks and a rock islet offshore.
- **Still open:** Nova's second Pokémon (needed by phase 3), what the keeper's cottage is for (by phase 4), and the headland's and lighthouse's names.

## Context
- Carnrudh's south-west path runs down x 6–7 to its bottom edge (rows 26–29), and Route 2 joins it with a `down` connection (offset -4; Route 2's road is x 10–11). The game draws the neighbouring map across a connection with the *current* map's tilesets, so Route 2 shares Carnrudh's `gTileset_General` + `gTileset_Dewford`, or keeps secondary-tileset blocks away from the join.
- The south-west road's blocker (Lumen survey traffic until the tor is done, see `design/Carnrudh.md`) was designed but left until this route existed. It reads `VAR_CARNRUDH_STATE` (`< TOR_DONE`), so it needs no new state.
- Layouts are generated by scripts in `tools/kernia/` with `layoutlib.py` (cliffs, stairs, trees, sand roads). It has no sea or shoreline helpers yet. Balwyn and Route 1 already use shore blocks; `map-building-tiles` memory lists the verified metatile IDs. Re-running a generator overwrites Porymap edits.
- No vanilla tileset has a lighthouse, so it's a stand-in until custom art (`design/CustomArt.md`).
- **Trainers:** `TRAINERS_COUNT_EMERALD` is 867 of `MAX_TRAINERS_COUNT_EMERALD` 896, so 29 IDs are free. This plan needs about 5–6 (route trainers, Nova, the optional headland trainers). No limit change.
- **Flags:** Kernia's own flags have three free slots left (0x2D–0x2F), and `flags.h` has many more `FLAG_UNUSED_*`. The rod uses vanilla's `FLAG_RECEIVED_OLD_ROD`, and Dewford's `DewfordTown_EventScript_GiveOldRod` is the pattern. Nova's first battle is `TRAINER_NOVA_BALWYN` (Pawmi Lv5, class Rival).
- Naming per CLAUDE.md: `KerniaRoute2` / `MAP_KERNIA_ROUTE2` / `LAYOUT_KERNIA_ROUTE2` in `gMapGroup_KerniaTownsAndRoutes`, region map section `MAPSEC_ROUTE_2` (shown as "ROUTE 2").
- Maps, scenes, and dialogue are tested by hand in mGBA (CLAUDE.md). Scenario state is tested with `make check TESTS="Scenario"`, and new story flags go into the matching scenario story part in the same phase (`tools/playtest/README.md`).

## Approach
Same as Carnrudh: generate the layout by script, copying vanilla block patterns so collision and elevation are right, render it to PNG for review, then fine-tune in Porymap. The route is one map: the terraced headland, with the lighthouse on its summit and the bay at its foot, and the way to Porthliw leaving west along the beach. Phase 1 proves the riskiest part, the coast layout and the join with Carnrudh, as a walkable route. Each later phase adds one thing the player meets on it. The route has no story var of its own: Nova's battle uses its trainer flag plus a hide flag, and the rod uses `FLAG_RECEIVED_OLD_ROD`.

- **Building Porthliw in the same plan:** the town's opening-night story is a separate piece of work, and Route 2 can be played and tested without it.
