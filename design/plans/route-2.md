# Route 2

## Goal
Build Route 2 as designed in `design/Route2.md`: the coast path south from Carnrudh over a terraced headland, with a lighthouse on its summit and a sandy bay at its foot. Nova battles during a Lumen photo shoot by the lighthouse, and a fisherman at the bay gives the player the Old Rod. It's a breather after Carnrudh's quest, with no Lumen beat. Porthliw is a separate plan; this one stops at the route's far edge.

## Context
- Carnrudh's south-west path runs down x 6–7 to its bottom edge (rows 26–29), and Route 2 joins it with a `down` connection (offset -4; Route 2's road is x 10–11). The game draws the neighbouring map across a connection with the *current* map's tilesets, so Route 2 shares Carnrudh's `gTileset_General` + `gTileset_Dewford`, or keeps secondary-tileset blocks away from the join.
- The south-west road's blocker (Lumen survey traffic until the tor is done, see `design/Carnrudh.md`) was designed but left until this route existed. It reads `VAR_CARNRUDH_STATE` (`< TOR_DONE`), so it needs no new state.
- Layouts are generated by scripts in `tools/kernia/` with `layoutlib.py` (cliffs, stairs, trees, sand roads). It has no sea or shoreline helpers; Route 2 didn't need them, because its generator copies Mossdeep's coast block for block. Re-running a generator overwrites Porymap edits.
- No vanilla tileset has a lighthouse, so it's a stand-in until custom art (`design/CustomArt.md`).
- **Trainers:** `TRAINERS_COUNT_EMERALD` is 867 of `MAX_TRAINERS_COUNT_EMERALD` 896, so 29 IDs are free. This plan needs about 5–6 (route trainers, Nova, the optional headland trainers). No limit change.
- **Flags:** Kernia's own flags have three free slots left (0x2D–0x2F), and `flags.h` has many more `FLAG_UNUSED_*`. The rod uses vanilla's `FLAG_RECEIVED_OLD_ROD`, and Dewford's `DewfordTown_EventScript_GiveOldRod` is the pattern. Nova's first battle is `TRAINER_NOVA_BALWYN` (Pawmi Lv5, class Rival).
- Naming per CLAUDE.md: `KerniaRoute2` / `MAP_KERNIA_ROUTE2` / `LAYOUT_KERNIA_ROUTE2` in `gMapGroup_KerniaTownsAndRoutes`, region map section `MAPSEC_ROUTE_2` (shown as "ROUTE 2").
- Maps, scenes, and dialogue are tested by hand in mGBA (CLAUDE.md). Scenario state is tested with `make check TESTS="Scenario"`, and new story flags go into the matching scenario story part in the same phase (`tools/playtest/README.md`).

## Approach
Same as Carnrudh: generate the layout by script, copying vanilla block patterns so collision and elevation are right, render it to PNG for review, then fine-tune in Porymap. The route is one map: the terraced headland, with the lighthouse on its summit and the bay at its foot, and the way to Porthliw leaving west along the beach. Phase 1 proved the riskiest part, the coast layout and the join with Carnrudh, as a walkable route. Each later phase adds one thing the player meets on it. The route has no story var of its own: Nova's battle uses its trainer flag plus a hide flag, and the rod uses `FLAG_RECEIVED_OLD_ROD`.
