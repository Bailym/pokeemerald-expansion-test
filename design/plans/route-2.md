# Route 2

## Goal
Build Route 2 as designed in `design/Route2.md`: the cliff-top coast path south-west from Carnrudh to a lighthouse headland, where Nova battles during a Lumen photo shoot and a fisherman at the seal cove gives the player the Old Rod. It's a breather after Carnrudh's quest, with no Lumen beat. Porthliw is a separate plan; this one stops at the route's far edge.

## Context
- Carnrudh's south-west path already runs down x 6–7 to its bottom edge (rows 26–29), reserved for this route, so Route 2 joins with a `down` connection. The game draws the neighbouring map across a connection with the *current* map's tilesets, so Route 2 shares Carnrudh's `gTileset_General` + `gTileset_Dewford`, or keeps secondary-tileset blocks away from the join.
- The south-west road's blocker (Lumen survey traffic until the tor is done, see `design/Carnrudh.md`) was designed but left until this route existed. It reads `VAR_CARNRUDH_STATE` (`< TOR_DONE`), so it needs no new state.
- Layouts are generated by scripts in `tools/kernia/` with `layoutlib.py` (cliffs, stairs, trees, sand roads). It has no sea or shoreline helpers yet. Balwyn and Route 1 already use shore blocks; `map-building-tiles` memory lists the verified metatile IDs. Re-running a generator overwrites Porymap edits.
- No vanilla tileset has a lighthouse, so it's a stand-in until custom art (`design/CustomArt.md`).
- **Trainers:** `TRAINERS_COUNT_EMERALD` is 867 of `MAX_TRAINERS_COUNT_EMERALD` 896, so 29 IDs are free. This plan needs about 5–6 (route trainers, Nova, the optional headland trainers). No limit change.
- **Flags:** Kernia's own flags have three free slots left (0x2D–0x2F), and `flags.h` has many more `FLAG_UNUSED_*`. The rod uses vanilla's `FLAG_RECEIVED_OLD_ROD`, and Dewford's `DewfordTown_EventScript_GiveOldRod` is the pattern. Nova's first battle is `TRAINER_NOVA_BALWYN` (Pawmi Lv5, class Rival).
- Naming per CLAUDE.md: `KerniaRoute2` / `MAP_KERNIA_ROUTE2` / `LAYOUT_KERNIA_ROUTE2` in `gMapGroup_KerniaTownsAndRoutes`, region map section `MAPSEC_ROUTE_2` (shown as "ROUTE 2").
- Maps, scenes, and dialogue are tested by hand in mGBA (CLAUDE.md). Scenario state is tested with `make check TESTS="Scenario"`, and new story flags go into the matching scenario story part in the same phase (`tools/playtest/README.md`).

## Approach
Same as Carnrudh: generate the layout by script, copying vanilla block patterns so collision and elevation are right, render it to PNG for review, then fine-tune in Porymap. The route is one map, with the headland and the seal cove at its south-west end and the way to Porthliw leaving from there. Phase 1 proves the riskiest part, the coast layout and the join with Carnrudh, as a walkable route. Each later phase adds one thing the player meets on it. The route has no story var of its own: Nova's battle uses its trainer flag plus a hide flag, and the rod uses `FLAG_RECEIVED_OLD_ROD`.

## Phases

### Phase 1 — Walk from Carnrudh to the headland
**Deliverable:** After the tor, the player walks south out of Carnrudh onto Route 2 with a "ROUTE 2" popup and no seam at the join. They follow the cliff-top path to the headland, with its stand-in lighthouse and the seal cove below, and wild battles come from the tall grass. Before the tor, a Lumen survey worker blocks Carnrudh's south-west exit.
**Verify:** `make -j$(nproc)` succeeds. Then in mGBA:
- `play.py carnrudh_arrived`: walk to the south-west exit and get turned back by the survey worker.
- `play.py carnrudh_badge1`: walk south. The popup appears, and the join has no garbled tiles.
- Walk the whole route to the headland and down into the cove. You can't walk onto the sea, the cliffs, or the lighthouse, and the far edge towards Porthliw is bounded.
- Tall grass gives wild battles, and open ground doesn't.

Also reopen Porymap and check the connection shows on both maps.
**Work:**
- `tools/kernia/gen_kernia_route2.py`: the layout with Carnrudh's tilesets. A path from Carnrudh's x 6–7 along the cliff top, with the sea to one side, some tall grass, and the headland at the far end. The cove sits below the cliffs, reached by stairs, with water to fish from later. Add sea and shore helpers to `layoutlib.py` if the generator needs them. Render to PNG and review, including the lighthouse stand-in.
- `layouts.json`, `data/maps/KerniaRoute2/map.json` (`MAPSEC_ROUTE_2`, route music, sunny weather), `scripts.inc`, the group entry, and the `up`/`down` connections between Carnrudh and Route 2.
- Carnrudh: the survey worker in the south-west exit, shown while `VAR_CARNRUDH_STATE` is below `TOR_DONE` and hidden after (a `FLAG_HIDE_*` set with the festival).
- `src/data/wild_encounters.json`: `gKerniaRoute2` land table, with coastal Flying and Water types alongside Route 1's species, around Lv9–12.
- A route sign. Map notes in `design/Route2.md`. Update `design/Carnrudh.md`'s connections and map notes.

**Not yet:** trainers, items, Nova, the fisherman and fishing, seals, and the connection to Porthliw.

Walking up to them (or into Nova's sight) starts a short scene: Nova plays to the cameras and battles the player with Pawmi plus a "tech" Pokémon at Lv13–14. Win or lose, the story carries on, Nova has a line for each, and then Nova and the crew leave the headland for good.
**Verify:**

### Phase 4 — The seal cove, the Old Rod, and the headland's trainers
**Deliverable:** In the cove below the headland, seals lie on the rocks and a fisherman gives the player the Old Rod. Fishing from the cove and along the shore gives water encounters. One or two optional trainers on the headland (a birdwatcher, a sailor) offer extra battles before Porthliw.
**Verify:**

- The seals react when talked to. The optional trainers battle once.

**Work:**

- One or two optional trainers on the headland, about Lv13.
- `design/Route2.md`: the cove, the rod, and the trainers. TestGuide: the cove checks.

**Not yet:** the connection to Porthliw (Porthliw's plan) and custom art for the lighthouse.

## Open questions
- **The lighthouse stand-in:** which vanilla building or object reads best as a lighthouse. Settled by the phase 1 render; the real lighthouse goes on `design/CustomArt.md`.
- **Nova's "tech" Pokémon** (Magnemite, Voltorb, or another) and Nova's lines. Needed by phase 3.
- **The headland's and lighthouse's Kernia names:** needed for the route sign and lines by phases 1–3, but placeholders that avoid real names are fine until then.
- **Where Route 2 meets Porthliw:** phase 1 bounds the far edge. Porthliw's plan picks the connection side and offset, so phase 1 should leave a clear path to whichever edge is likely (west or south).

## Rejected alternatives
- **The headland as its own map:** unlike Carn Kowr's peak, nothing here needs to run only on the headland, and one map keeps the route, cove, and headland in one generator. If the route gets too long for one map (about 86×86, `MAX_MAP_DATA_SIZE`), split it then.
- **A story var for Route 2:** Nova's trainer flag and one hide flag cover the only scene, and the rod has its own flag.
- **Building Porthliw in the same plan:** the town's opening-night story is a separate piece of work, and Route 2 can be played and tested without it.
